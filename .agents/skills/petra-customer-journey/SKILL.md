Use the five stages in the [buyer journey](../../../data/customer-journey.md). Start with quality or sampling problems and work toward comparing manufacturing models, shortlisting suppliers, and preparing a brief.

Review destinations against the existing content map. Use dated observations for any gap. Record optional AI checks in the [manual observations file](../../../data/ai-visibility.json) with provider, prompt ID, market, date and outcome. Keep raw answers and private accounts out of the store.

Done when each question names the buyer's decision and a reviewable destination. Adapted from [Illyism's customer-journey method](https://github.com/Illyism/seo-skills).
