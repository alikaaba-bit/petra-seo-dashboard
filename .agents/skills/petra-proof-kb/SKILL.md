Use [editorial.json](../../../data/editorial.json) as the fact and proof store. Keep unknown facts null. Use one stable ID for each approved receipt with its source, review date, category, and exact page scope.

- Ask operations to confirm the public wording and limits for tests, permitted client outcomes, original photos or videos, aggregate numbers, and sampling or QC edge cases.
- Reference a dated, public-safe source. Treat outreach notes, historical drafts and observed website claims as research input.
- Give the page opening one documented first-hand receipt. Aim for four distinct proof categories in the single-page sprint and leave missing categories held.

Done when the public candidate passes the [reviewed import and rebuild](../../../README.md#record-facts-proof-and-manual-observations) and every included claim has a source. Adapted from [Illyism's proof-first method](https://github.com/Illyism/seo-skills).
