Read the repository's [operating rules](../../../AGENTS.md). Build the [roadmap](../../../data/roast-roadmap.md) with the existing workspace script. Check the crawl, each required route, and GSC attempt and snapshot dates separately.

1. Put verified crawl, indexability and measurement blockers first. A missing observation needs a check. A previously broken URL with fresh evidence goes in the roadmap as verified.
2. Review revenue pages for buyer intent, proof and a clear enquiry step. Follow with contextual links and visibility research. Defer polish.
3. Name the owner and the next decision. For a broken live guide, scope a human decision to restore it at its approved path or retarget to a verified guide.

Done when the dated roadmap and Weekly review reflect the same evidence. Adapted from [Illyism's SEO workflows](https://github.com/Illyism/seo-skills).
