Use authorized public evidence and the current [buyer journey](../../../data/customer-journey.md). Identify the buyer's job, decision, concern and the evidence that could answer it. Record whether language came from a live supplier SERP, a public source or a working hypothesis.

Use this material in the relevant page review. Ask the operations owner before turning any MOQ, timing, material or quality note into a public claim. Keep client identifiers and raw enquiries in their private systems.

Done when the page's buyer problem and next action are specific and supported by the recorded evidence. Adapted from [Illyism's buyer-research workflows](https://github.com/Illyism/seo-skills).
