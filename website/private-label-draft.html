From design selection to production approval

1. Review the design direction

Request a catalogue or share reference pieces. Discuss which designs fit your brand and what can be adapted. Confirm material, finish, size, and branding requirements before moving to samples.

2. Define branding and packaging

Share your logo files, packaging direction, and labeling requirements. Ask the team to confirm available options and any changes to pricing, minimum quantities, or lead times.

3. Review and approve samples

Use the sample stage to check the details that matter to your customer: appearance, dimensions, finish, branding, and packaging. Record requested changes and approve the final specification before production begins.

4. Confirm production and delivery requirements

Agree on quantities, quality checks, packaging, delivery destination, and the production schedule. Keep the approved specification as the reference for the order.

Questions to confirm before placing an order

What is the minimum order quantity?

Ask for the minimum quantity for each design and material in your proposed collection. Include your expected order size so the team can confirm which options fit.

How long do samples and production take?

Request separate sample and production estimates for your project. Confirm when each stage starts, what approvals are required, and how design changes affect the schedule.

How do we agree on quality requirements?

Discuss the inspection criteria and supporting documentation your brand needs. Use the approved samples and written specifications to make expectations clear before production.

What if we need a completely custom design?

If your project starts with an original design rather than an existing collection, review our contract manufacturing service and share your design files with the team.
